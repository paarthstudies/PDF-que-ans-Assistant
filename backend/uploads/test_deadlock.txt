Deadlock in Operating Systems

Deadlock is a condition where two or more processes are unable to proceed because each is waiting for a resource held by another process in the set.

Four necessary conditions for deadlock:
1. Mutual Exclusion - resources cannot be shared
2. Hold and Wait - a process holds resources while waiting for others
3. No Preemption - resources cannot be forcibly taken away
4. Circular Wait - a circular chain of processes waiting for each other

Prevention strategies include breaking one of the four conditions. Detection and recovery allow the system to identify deadlocks and resolve them by aborting or preempting processes.
